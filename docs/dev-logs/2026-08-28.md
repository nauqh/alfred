# 2026-08-28

## 📜 Queue is now paginated

A long queue used to be one giant list. Alfred now shows the current track and the next 10,
with Prev / Next buttons to flip through the rest. The numbering keeps counting across pages,
so track 11 is still "11." on page two.

## ⏱️ The progress bar keeps moving

The bar under "Now Playing" now catches up every 15 seconds instead of freezing where it was
when the card went up. A paused track and a live stream are left alone - they have no bar
that moves.

## 👥 More people can press the buttons

Previously only the bot owner could press Pause, Skip or Loop on the now playing card. Now
the person who queued the current song can control it too - as long as they are in the same
voice channel. The right to control belongs to the track, not the whole queue: once the song
moves on, so does the control.

## 🔎 Ask Alfred to search from chat

@mention Alfred with "search for..." and it digs up the top 5 matches, numbered, with links.
Reply with a number - "play 2" - and it queues that one. Nothing is queued until you pick;
searching is just looking.

## 🎵 Track name in the sidebar

Alfred used to sit under a static "Listening to <landing page>" status. Now, while a track
plays, the little status under its name shows the actual song and artist, like
"Listening to Never Gonna Give You Up - Rick Astley". When the queue finishes it goes back to
the normal message. Handy when you want to know what is on without opening Discord audio.

## 🖥️ Own YouTube cipher server

YouTube signs its audio URLs in a way the music source can no longer read on its own, so
everyone who streams from YouTube now runs a little helper server that decrypts those URLs.
Alfred deploys and points at its own copy of that helper (`yt-cipher`) instead of relying on
a shared public one. Nothing to configure by hand, and it fixes a whole class of "can't play
this video" failures for good.
